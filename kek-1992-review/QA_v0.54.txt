NE(X)T NATIVE TERMINAL EXCEPTION
- Historical target remains Ohlfs 10pt.
- Original Ohlfs bytes are not bundled.
- The project decision remains to use a separate reconstruction rather than relabel Courier/X11 fixed as Ohlfs.
- CERN 2019 documents pixel-traced reconstruction from a real NeXT display; NEXTSPACE documents an independently created Keith.nfont similar to Ohlfs.
- v0.54 does not silently redistribute third-party reconstructed font bytes without an unambiguous per-font redistribution grant.
- Therefore the native NeXT exact/reconstructed glyph asset is the one remaining font item not certified/pinned in this package.
- The native NeXT profile is fail-closed: no Courier/system fallback is drawn as historical content; an out-of-frame status notice explains why the text plane is intentionally not rendered.

This ZIP includes the complete x3270 4.1ga10 x3270/3270.bdf as the local runtime
asset, without reducing it to an ASCII subset. The bundled BDF declares 431
glyphs, a 9x14 font bounding box, CHARSET_REGISTRY 3270cg, and
CHARSET_ENCODING 1a. Its SHA-256 is pinned as:
3d71ea77ade51a7d2d6de326bd98fd14724b0e0086ecc0bd2d5083483d34838b

The emulator reads this local file only; it does not fetch the 3270 font from
the network at runtime. ASCII display characters are mapped through STARTCHAR
names because the BDF follows 3270 character-generator ordering rather than
normal ASCII code-point ordering.
